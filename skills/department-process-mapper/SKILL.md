# Department Process Mapper
1. Establish scope, geography, organization size and known systems.
2. Separate confirmed facts, assumptions and unknowns.
3. Identify functions, roles, recurring processes, triggers, inputs, outputs and handoffs.
4. Record systems, sensitivity, approvals, exceptions and owners.
5. Ask only questions that materially change the map.
6. Return department_summary, functions[], processes[], systems[], controls[], unknowns[], evidence_refs[].
Do not invent structure, responsibilities or volumes. Label hypotheses.
