# Research & Trend Intelligence
## Domains
Corporate processes; open-source repositories; academic research; APIs/models/SaaS; market use cases; internal execution telemetry.

## Source priority
Official docs and release notes; maintainer repositories; standards and primary research; reputable technical publications; community sources as leads only.

## Record
Canonical URL, publisher, source type, published/retrieved timestamps, content hash, version, license, excerpt, claim, confidence, verification status and tenant visibility.

## Watch signals
New releases/deprecations, API/schema changes, security advisories, pricing/context changes, new integrations/templates, emerging automation patterns and internal failure/cost changes.

## Baseline cadence
Daily release/change signals; weekly department/niche rotation; monthly coverage and stale-finding review. Configurable per tenant and source limits.

Deduplicate by canonical URL, repository identity, content hash and semantic similarity. Preserve history. A trend score prioritizes review; it is not proof of business value.
