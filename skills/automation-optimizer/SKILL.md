# Automation Optimizer
1. Retrieve representative baseline window.
2. Segment failures, latency, cost, volume and interventions.
3. Identify root-cause hypotheses with evidence.
4. Rank proposals by benefit, confidence, risk and effort.
5. Define sandbox experiment and success metrics.
6. Include change diff, rollback and approval.
Return baseline, observations, proposals, experiment and rollback plan. Never change production or infer ROI from biased samples.
