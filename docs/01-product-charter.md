# Product Charter
## Vision
An evidence-driven corporate intelligence platform that maps organizational work, discovers automation opportunities, researches solutions, assembles workflows and safely operates approved automations.

## Outcomes
1. Map departments, functions, processes, systems and controls.
2. Identify opportunities with evidence, assumptions and impact ranges.
3. Maintain a versioned registry of n8n nodes, APIs, tools, MCP servers, Skills and templates.
4. Translate natural-language requests into testable specifications.
5. Generate workflow drafts from exact schemas and reusable patterns.
6. Guide credential/configuration setup without exposing secrets to models.
7. Validate in an isolated sandbox.
8. Deploy only after authorization.
9. Measure reliability, cost, time saved and business outcomes.
10. Propose improvements based on telemetry.

## Modes
- Scheduled research: coverage-gap and change-signal driven.
- On-demand: investigate, clarify, plan, build, test and route for approval.

## Roles
Platform owner, organization admin, department owner, automation builder, auditor, requester.

## MVP non-goals
Unrestricted production autonomy; foundation-model training; arbitrary repository-code execution; unsupported ROI claims.

## MVP acceptance
A department research task produces source-linked processes and scored opportunities. An automation request produces a structured specification, exact node inventory, valid draft, configuration manifest and test report. Production deployment is approval-gated and auditable.
